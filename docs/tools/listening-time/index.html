How the numbers are worked out

Audiobook and podcast runtimes are listed at 1x speed, so your actual listening time is what's left divided by your speed. A 12 hours 30 minutes book at 1.25x takes 10 hours (12.5 ÷ 1.25). We subtract your current position before dividing so only the unheard part gets sped up, then round the output to the nearest minute.

Your finish date counts days starting today assuming you hit your daily minutes every day. Pick a finish-by date and the calculator counts every day from today through that date (both ends included), then divides the remaining book minutes by your total listening minutes to get the speed you'll need. It all runs locally in your browser; nothing you enter leaves the page.

Worked examples

I checked each example below in this calculator in Chrome on 24 November 2026; punch any of them into the form above to verify the same numbers.

  • 45 minutes at 1.75x speed: set length to 0 h 45 min, position to 0 h 0 min, speed to 1.75x. 45 divided by 1.75 is 25.71 minutes, or 25 minutes and 43 seconds (19 minutes and 17 seconds saved, 43% off), which rounds to 26 min.
  • 6-hour audiobook at 1.5x speed: enter 6 h 0 min at 1.5x. 360 minutes divided by 1.5 gives 240 minutes, or 4 h flat (2 hours saved, 33% shorter; 4 days at 60 minutes a day).
  • 12 h 30 min book from 3 h 15 min at 1.25x (default inputs): you're 26.0% done with 9 h 15 min (555 minutes) left at normal speed. At 1.25x that's 7 h 24 min, or 10 days at 45 minutes a day (10 h for the whole book).
  • 10-hour book from 2 h 0 min with a 7-day deadline at 45 min a day: 8 hours (480 minutes) left across 315 listening minutes over 7 days. 480 divided by 315 gives 1.52x when you set Finish by to six days from today.

What a faster speed saves

Each bump in speed trims less time than the last, which is why most people park somewhere between 1.25x and 1.75x.

Questions

How long is 45 minutes at 1.75x speed?

25 minutes and 43 seconds (45 divided by 1.75 is 25.71 minutes), saving 19 minutes and 17 seconds. Because the calculator rounds to the nearest minute, it shows 26 min.

How do I work out how long an audiobook takes at 1.5x?

Divide the normal-speed runtime by 1.5: a 6-hour book takes 4 hours and a 12-hour book takes 8 hours. At 2x, they drop to 3 hours and 6 hours.

Where do I find the position and the length?

Your player shows both right on the playback screen in ABS+ and other Audiobookshelf apps, or under the cover in the Audiobookshelf web player. Enter the normal-speed length rather than the speed-adjusted time left.

Why does my player show a different time left?

Some players scale the time left by your current speed while others stick to normal-speed time. Put normal-speed length and position in here and let the calculator do the division.

What speed do I need to finish a book by a date?

Divide the normal-speed minutes left by the total minutes you'll listen from today through your deadline (counting both days). Pick a date in Finish by and the calculator handles it.

Listen on your own Audiobookshelf server

ABS+ is a small, free, open-source Android and iPhone player for your own Audiobookshelf server, built with offline downloads, six playback speeds (0.8x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x and 2x) and server progress sync.
